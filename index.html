Recommended workflow: use CFBD's Data Exporter to pull a CSV (play-by-play, PPA/success-rate splits with garbage time excluded, etc.), then upload the CSV in chat — I'll fold the numbers into the model. No key ever has to leave CFBD's site.

Power Ratings

Model uses ESPN SP+ (points, drives the Board's spreads). FEI, FPI, and Sagarin ranks are shown for reference/comparison only — they don't feed the model. FEI = BCF Toys' Fremeau Efficiency Index, opponent-adjusted possession efficiency with garbage time already excluded (all 138 FBS teams). FPI = ESPN's preseason Top 25 (published Jul 9, 2026). Sagarin = Jeff Sagarin's 2026 "starting" rating (sagarin.com) — despite being pulled after Week 0, the source explicitly labels these preseason seed values (every team shows a 0-0 record); treat as a baseline, not a live in-season number yet. Will update once his site starts reflecting actual results. (PFF's power rankings live behind a JS-rendered tool I can't scrape reliably, so that column's been dropped. Pick Six's ranking was also dropped since it won't update in-season.)
